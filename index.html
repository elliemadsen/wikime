      About

      As I found myself increasingly wandering Wikipedia during various processes of (loosely-defined) research, I began saving pages I frequently visited or wished to bookmark in an Are.na channel. I created this website to spatialize this archive of interests. Pages are closer together when more Wikipedia links connect them. The Pathfinder allows me to discover the most direct linked path between concepts, while the Timeline replays the order in which I saved them.
